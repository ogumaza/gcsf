GCSF 1.13.0 rips the music of Luigi's Mansion's boss fights in each state that the game plays, LuigiSings without Luigi's humming, and the piano piece that the mansion plays near the pianist's room.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game code on an emulated GameCube: the OS, the sound library and the DSP program of the game. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `gcsf-1.13.0-win-x64.zip` | `gcsfrip` and `gcsfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_gcsf-1.13.0.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-gcsf-1.13.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.13.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New in 1.13.0

- **Luigi's Mansion's boss music in each state.** The music of four boss fights (`BabyBattle`, `BlueBattle`, `TelesaBattle` and `KuppaBattle`) changes its tracks as the fight goes on, and for some of them its tempo. A rip now contains a variant for each later state, such as `BabyBattle state 1`: states 1 and 2 of BabyBattle and BlueBattle, and state 1 of TelesaBattle and KuppaBattle. Rip Luigi's Mansion again to get these variants.
- **LuigiSings without the humming.** While a message window is open, the game mutes Luigi's humming, and the accompaniment plays alone. A rip now contains that at 100 HP in the hallway, a room and outdoors, such as `LuigiSings hallway 100 HP no humming`.
- **The piano piece of Luigi's Mansion.** `se1-0169` is the piano piece that the mansion plays while Luigi is in the hallway next to the pianist's room. The game's sound table lists it as an effect. `--bgm` now rips it with the music.

## Fixed in 1.13.0

- **Luigi's Mansion's boss music.** A rip of `BabyBattle`, `BlueBattle`, `TelesaBattle` or `KuppaBattle` played every track of the piece at once: the music of all its states together. The ripper now starts each piece of Luigi's Mansion through the game's start of its music, which reads the piece's entry in the sound table and applies the piece's first state. The file of each boss piece now plays the music as the fight starts.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two passes of their loop and a fade when the ripper finds the loop. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end. The analysis plays music for up to an hour and a sound effect for up to ten minutes. A sound still playing at that limit gets a length of ten minutes and a fade.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams. Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike streams all of its music.
- The ripper leaves out the effects of one voice file of Tales of Symphonia. The file has no pool. MusyX can't load its group without one.
- GCSF doesn't rip the sound effects of Resident Evil. The code of a room changes the channel levels of some songs while they play, above all those of the ambiences. A rip plays each song at the levels in its file, apart from the six mixes.
- A rip plays each effect of Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes and Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door with the settings that the game's table gives it. The game places its effects in the world and can change their volumes and pans as they play. A rip plays each effect of Rebel Strike at the volume and the pan of its group, without the reverb that a level gives it.
- The code of Tales of Symphonia and Rogue Leader can give an effect a pan. A rip plays each of their effects in the centre. Star Fox Adventures sends its effects to a reverb in some places of its world. Resident Evil Zero sends its effects to an aux bus. A rip plays the effects of both games without those sends.
- 19 effects of Rebel Strike jump back through GOTO steps of their macros. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Some effects of Tales of Symphonia and Rogue Leader keep going until the game stops them. The ripper finds no loop in them. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Four effects of Metroid Prime and one of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes play only some of the times that the game starts them. In a rip, each one makes a random choice that silences it. The ripper skips it. One more effect of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes, `SFXspb_a_needlexp_00_oneshot`, tests a variable of the MusyX macros. In a rip, the test skips its sample. The ripper skips the effect.
- Eight songs of Rogue Leader play a stream along with sequenced notes: The Battle Of Yavin I and III, Hyperspace, Imperial Attack, The Asteroid Field, Celebration & End Titles, and two songs that the music hall doesn't list. A rip of one of them would play only its notes. The ripper leaves them out.
- Some cutscene songs of Resident Evil Zero start with a rest that times the music to the scene. A rip plays the rest. `bgm_78` is silent for 53 s before its music starts, `bgm_79` for 19 s and `bgm_88` for 16 s.
- The continuous sounds of Animal Crossing and its Japanese releases, such as rivers, the sea and insects, aren't ripped. Neither are the villagers' voices.
- The program on the Bonus Disc contains a second set of 825 effects that it never loads. GCSF doesn't rip them.
- Some effects of The Wind Waker read the reverb of the room that they play in. A few of them make no sound in a rip with any value. The ripper skips them. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- A rip plays `LuigiSings` in 20 states of Luigi's Mansion. Each one takes the most health of its band. In the game, the tempo falls further as Luigi loses health within a band. While the lights are on, the tempo follows Luigi's steps. A rip of the lit room plays the tempo of Luigi standing still.
- In Luigi's Mansion, `TelesaBattle` plays faster as Luigi catches the small Boos of the fight. `TelesaBattle state 1` plays the tempo of the first time the fight reaches that state.
- 10 effects of Super Mario Sunshine loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- In Donkey Kong Jungle Beat, the keys of a chord effect depend on the stage, the chord of the moment and the keys before. A rip plays each chord effect in one state: over the music of one stage, from the first of the game's records that names it. Some chord effects play no notes in that state. The ripper skips them. Some others repeat their steps until the game stops them. They play for ten minutes, then fade. Later in a combo, the game moves some chord effects on by more steps. A rip plays only the steps that start by themselves. Some effects that aren't chord effects take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them. They play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them.
- A rip of a state of Jungle Beat's music plays that state from the start of the piece. The game moves from one state to the next with a short fade. The rips leave out those changes.
- 16 level sounds of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, the ramps of the engines, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star music and the Chain Chomp music, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Some effects of Pikmin loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade. A rip plays the formation sound for one move only: a full move straight ahead. The chatter of the Pikmin follows the state of the game through values that the game sets. The ripper leaves it out.
- Pikmin 2 plays some pieces through a conductor, such as the music of its caves. The conductor picks the phrases at random. Such a piece never repeats. Its rip plays for ten minutes, then fades.
- Twilight Princess HD is missing one of the wave archives of the Wii release. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
