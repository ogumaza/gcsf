GCSF 1.6.0 rips four more games: Pokémon Channel, Mario Party 4, Nintendo Puzzle Collection and the NDDEMO tech demo. Rips of the other games are as with 1.5.1.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game's code on an emulated GameCube: the game's OS, its sound library and its DSP program. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `gcsf-1.6.0-win-x64.zip` | `gcsfrip` and `gcsfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_gcsf-1.6.0.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-gcsf-1.6.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.6.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New games

- **Pokémon Channel** (USA): its 38 pieces of music and its sound effects. The game plays each piece of music as a MusyX effect that plays a stereo recording. GCSF rips them as music. A rip names a sound as the game's sound viewer does, such as `SND_SUOU`. A sound that the viewer doesn't name takes its set and its ID, such as `pchsound-1064`. The TV programmes' themes play as sound effects. A rip lists them with the effects.
- **Mario Party 4** (USA, disc version 1): the 76 songs that the sound test lists. A rip names a song as the sound test does, such as `Try Everything`. The game runs MusyX through Hudson's sound manager.
- **Nintendo Puzzle Collection** (Japan): the 118 songs of its menu and its three games, Dr. Mario, Panel de Pon and Yoshi's Cookie. A song's name starts with `menu`, `dr_mario`, `panepon` or `ycookie`. Dr. Mario's and Panel de Pon's songs then take the games' names for them, such as `panepon-flower_01`. The menu's songs take their IDs, such as `menu-01`. Yoshi's Cookie's songs take the names of their files, such as `ycookie-sng05`.
- **The NDDEMO tech demo**: its nine songs. The demo is a GameCube disc that never went on sale. A rip names a song after its file, such as `coins`.

## Other changes

- **Discs without the magic word.** GCSF reads a GameCube disc whose header leaves out the magic word, if the rest of the disc is intact. The NDDEMO disc is one of them.
- **The libraries.** GCSF now builds on ntdlib 1.9.0, with xsflib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams.
- The sound effects of Tales of Symphonia, Star Fox Adventures, Rogue Leader, Pokémon Colosseum and Resident Evil Zero aren't ripped. Neither are the jingles of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door. The game plays them as songs of MusyX. Rebel Strike's songs aren't ripped either.
- The sound effects of Mario Party 4, Nintendo Puzzle Collection and the NDDEMO tech demo aren't ripped. Neither are Mario Party 4's voices.
- A rip plays each of the effects of Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes and Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door with the settings that the game's table gives it. The game places its effects in the world and can change their volumes and pans as they play. A rip plays each of Rebel Strike's effects at the volume and the pan of its group, without the reverb that a level gives it.
- 19 of Rebel Strike's effects jump back through GOTO steps of their macros. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Four of Metroid Prime's effects and one of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes' play only some of the times that the game starts them. In a rip, each one makes a random choice that silences it. The ripper skips it. One more of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes' effects, `SFXspb_a_needlexp_00_oneshot`, tests a variable of MusyX's macros. In a rip, the test skips its sample. The ripper skips the effect.
- Eight of Rogue Leader's songs play a stream along with sequenced notes: The Battle Of Yavin I and III, Hyperspace, Imperial Attack, The Asteroid Field, Celebration & End Titles, and two songs that the music hall doesn't list. A rip of one of them would play only its notes. The ripper leaves them out.
- Some of Resident Evil Zero's cutscene songs start with a rest that times the music to the scene. A rip plays the rest. `bgm_78` is silent for 53 s before its music starts, `bgm_79` for 19 s and `bgm_88` for 16 s.
- Animal Crossing's continuous sounds, such as rivers, the sea and insects, aren't ripped. Neither are the villagers' voices.
- The Bonus Disc's program holds a second set of 825 effects that it never loads. GCSF doesn't rip them.
- 34 of The Wind Waker's effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Each one reads a value that the game sets when it starts the effect, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- 10 of Super Mario Sunshine's effects loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- One of Luigi's Mansion's effects, se2-0239, asks for an instrument that its bank lacks. The game then reads past the end of the bank's list of instruments. Playback of its rip stops with an error at once.
- 181 of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's sound effects loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. 70 of its effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Most of them take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them.
- 872 of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'s sound effects are voices. The game keeps their samples in separate archives and loads one of those at a time. A rip loads none of them. These effects play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them. 32 of the game's effects, such as the engines and the crowds, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star's and the Chain Chomp's, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Three of Pikmin 2's effects play only some of the times that the game starts them: a Wollywog's voice and two of the Giant Breadbug's sounds. In a rip, each one draws a random number that silences it, and the ripper skips it.
- Twilight Princess HD lacks one of the Wii's wave archives. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
