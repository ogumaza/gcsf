GCSF 1.8.0 rips Doubutsu no Mori+ and Doubutsu no Mori e+. They're the Japanese releases of Animal Crossing. GCSF also rips the sound effects of Tales of Symphonia, Star Fox Adventures, Rogue Leader, Resident Evil Zero, Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour, Mario Power Tennis and Nintendo Puzzle Collection. Each effect of The Wind Waker that reads a value from the game now rips as a variant for each value. The effects of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat that play in the chords of the music now rip over the music of a stage. The formation sound of Pikmin now rips too. A rip of one effect of Animal Crossing with random variants now plays the effect that its number names. Other rips play as they did with 1.7.0.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game code on an emulated GameCube: the OS, the sound library and the DSP program of the game. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.8.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New in 1.8.0

- **Doubutsu no Mori+.** Doubutsu no Mori+ is the Japanese release of Animal Crossing. GCSF now rips disc version 1 of it. Its rips play through the driver for Animal Crossing. They name the music and the sound effects as the rips of Animal Crossing do, for example `bgm046` and `se2-017`. Animal Crossing plays numbers 83 to 87 as effects of the start-up screen. Doubutsu no Mori+ plays them as five short pieces of music. Its rips include those pieces.
- **Doubutsu no Mori e+.** Doubutsu no Mori e+ is a later Japanese release of the game. GCSF now rips disc version 1 of it. Its sound library is a later build that numbers the pieces with 16 bits. The game mutes six channels of the island piece unless the islander is on the island. A rip contains that piece twice: `bgm376-islander` with the islander on the island, and `bgm376-no-islander` without.
- **The sound effects of Tales of Symphonia.** GCSF now rips the sound effects and the voices of Tales of Symphonia. They follow the songs. A rip names an effect after its file and its ID, for example `se-0012`.
- **The sound effects of Star Fox Adventures.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of Star Fox Adventures. They follow the songs. The rows of effects in the game can set several volumes for an effect. A rip plays the effect at the volume that most of its rows set. A rip names an effect after its set and its ID, for example `starfoxs-0420`.
- **The sound effects of Rogue Leader.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader. They follow the songs. A rip plays an effect at the volume that most of the rows for it in the game set. A rip names an effect after its ID, for example `RogueLeader-0048`.
- **The sound effects of Resident Evil Zero.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of Resident Evil Zero on both discs. The effects of its cutscenes are left out. The others follow the songs. An effect plays at the volume and the pan of its row in the table of its group. A rip names an effect after its archive and its ID, for example `sh00-1467`.
- **The sound effects of Mario Golf and Mario Power Tennis.** GCSF now rips the sound effects, voices and narrations of Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour and Mario Power Tennis. They follow the songs and jingles. A rip names an effect after its voice bank and its ID, for example `mario-0154`. An effect of the bank that the game loads at start-up is named `se`, for example `se-0413`. So is an effect of the other groups of Mario Power Tennis. The name of a narration starts with `narration`, for example `narration-0362`. An effect of a court in Mario Power Tennis is named `court`, for example `court-0018`.
- **The sound effects of Nintendo Puzzle Collection.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of the menu and the three games of Nintendo Puzzle Collection. They follow the songs. A rip plays each effect as its module starts it. The name of an effect is the name of its module, `-se-` and the ID of the effect, for example `panepon-se-0012`.
- **The effects of The Wind Waker that read a value.** Some effects of The Wind Waker read a value that the game gives them when it starts them. One such value is the material of the ground under a footstep. They played nothing in a rip. The ripper skipped them. A rip now contains a variant of each such effect for each value that plays other samples. Values that play the same samples share one variant. The title of a variant is the name of the effect, a space and the value, for example `se3-0000 6=4`. A new tag, `gcsf_var`, contains the value. `docs/gcsf.md` describes it. A player plays another variant when the tag contains another value. The foobar2000 component keeps the tag when it rewrites the tags of a file. The XMPlay plugin leaves it out of the Message window. Earlier players ignore the tag. A variant then plays nothing in them.
- **The chord effects of Jungle Beat.** Donkey Kong Jungle Beat plays many of its effects in the chords of the music. The chord code of the game sets the keys of such an effect at the start of each step. The ripper used to skip most of these effects as silent. A rip now plays each of them through the chord code of the game. The effect plays over the music of one stage. The tracks of the music still run and supply the chords and beats of the effect. They play no notes. Three chord effects ripped before without their chord notes. In the state that a rip plays, they play no notes, and the ripper now skips them.
- **The formation sound of Pikmin.** Pikmin plays the sound of the formation while the player moves the group. The code of the game gives the sound the direction and the length of the move. The ripper used to leave the sound out. A rip now contains it as it plays for a full move of the formation straight ahead. The `gcsf_var` tag of the rip contains that move, `3=127`.

## Fixed in 1.8.0

- **The effects of Animal Crossing with random variants.** For some effects, the code of Animal Crossing adds a random number to the number of the effect. The game then plays the effect of the sum. A rip of such an effect played the variant that the time base of the emulated console picked. That was often the next effect. Each rip now plays the effect that its number names. The other variants have rips of their own. The rips of Doubutsu no Mori+ and Doubutsu no Mori e+ play their effects the same way.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams. Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike streams all of its music.
- The ripper leaves out the sound effects of the cutscenes in Resident Evil Zero. The ripper also leaves out the effects of one voice file of Tales of Symphonia. That file contains no pool of samples. MusyX can't load its group.
- A rip plays each effect of Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes and Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door with the settings that the game's table gives it. The game places its effects in the world and can change their volumes and pans as they play. A rip plays each effect of Rebel Strike at the volume and the pan of its group, without the reverb that a level gives it.
- The code of Tales of Symphonia and Rogue Leader can give an effect a pan. A rip plays each of their effects in the centre. Star Fox Adventures sends its effects to a reverb in some places of its world. Resident Evil Zero sends its effects to an aux bus. A rip plays the effects of both games without those sends.
- 19 effects of Rebel Strike jump back through GOTO steps of their macros. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Some effects of Tales of Symphonia and Rogue Leader keep going until the game stops them. The ripper finds no loop in them. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Four effects of Metroid Prime and one of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes play only some of the times that the game starts them. In a rip, each one makes a random choice that silences it. The ripper skips it. One more effect of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes, `SFXspb_a_needlexp_00_oneshot`, tests a variable of the MusyX macros. In a rip, the test skips its sample. The ripper skips the effect.
- Eight songs of Rogue Leader play a stream along with sequenced notes: The Battle Of Yavin I and III, Hyperspace, Imperial Attack, The Asteroid Field, Celebration & End Titles, and two songs that the music hall doesn't list. A rip of one of them would play only its notes. The ripper leaves them out.
- Some cutscene songs of Resident Evil Zero start with a rest that times the music to the scene. A rip plays the rest. `bgm_78` is silent for 53 s before its music starts, `bgm_79` for 19 s and `bgm_88` for 16 s.
- The continuous sounds of Animal Crossing and its Japanese releases, such as rivers, the sea and insects, aren't ripped. Neither are the villagers' voices.
- The program on the Bonus Disc contains a second set of 825 effects that it never loads. GCSF doesn't rip them.
- Some effects of The Wind Waker read the reverb of the room that they play in. A few of them make no sound in a rip with any value. The ripper skips them. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- 10 effects of Super Mario Sunshine loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- 181 sound effects of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. The keys of a chord effect depend on the stage, the chord of the moment and the keys before. A rip plays each chord effect in one state: over the music of one stage, from the first of the game's records that names it. Some chord effects play no notes in that state. The ripper skips them. Some others repeat their steps until the game stops them. They play for ten minutes, then fade. Later in a combo, the game moves some chord effects on by more steps. A rip plays only the steps that start by themselves. Some effects that aren't chord effects take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them. They play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them.
- 32 sound effects of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, such as the engines and the crowds, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star music and the Chain Chomp music, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Three effects of Pikmin 2 play only some of the times that the game starts them: the voice of a Wollywog and two sounds of the Giant Breadbug. In a rip, each one draws a random number that silences it, and the ripper skips it.
- Some effects of Pikmin loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade. A rip plays the formation sound for one move only: a full move straight ahead. The chatter of the Pikmin follows the state of the game through values that the game sets. The ripper leaves it out.
- Twilight Princess HD is missing one of the wave archives of the Wii release. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
