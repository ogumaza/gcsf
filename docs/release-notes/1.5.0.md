GCSF 1.5.0 rips eleven more games: Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes, Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, Tales of Symphonia, Star Fox Adventures, Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader, Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike, Pokémon Colosseum, Resident Evil Zero, Animal Crossing and the Pokémon Colosseum Bonus Disc. The first nine use the sound library MusyX. One new driver plays all nine through MusyX's functions. Animal Crossing and the Bonus Disc each have a new driver too. GCSF 1.3.0's player, foobar2000 component and XMPlay plugin play every rip that 1.5.0 makes.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game's code on an emulated GameCube: the game's OS, its sound library and its DSP program. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `gcsf-1.5.0-win-x64.zip` | `gcsfrip` and `gcsfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_gcsf-1.5.0.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-gcsf-1.5.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.5.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New games

- **Metroid Prime** (USA, disc version 2): its four sequenced songs and 2,143 sound effects. The game streams the rest of its music. GCSF doesn't rip streams. A rip names a song after its resource in the game's pack, such as `Main_Plaza`. A rip names an effect after its audio group set and its ID, such as `Scarab-0721`. A rip's library (`.gcsflib`) holds the songs and every audio group set of the game, 20.8 MB.
- **Metroid Prime 2: Echoes** (USA): 3,301 sound effects. The game streams its music. A rip names an effect as the game's table of names does, such as `SFXfnt_x_titlemusic_lp_00L_looped`. A rip's library holds every audio group set of the game, 60.2 MB.
- **Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door** (USA): 1,374 sound effects. The game streams its music. A rip names an effect as the game's table of sounds does, such as `SFX_PRESS_START1`. An effect plays at the volume, pan, pitch and send to the reverb that the table gives it. A rip's library holds the game's MusyX files, 12.3 MB.
- **Tales of Symphonia** (USA): 111 songs. Both discs hold the same music, and a rip of either disc gives the same set. A rip names a song after its file, such as `bgm_t000`. Each song gets the reverb that the game sets for it. A rip's library holds the songs and the music's group, 3.1 MB.
- **Star Fox Adventures** (USA, disc version 1): 99 songs. A rip names a song as the game's code does, such as `starfox_map`. A song plays at the volume that the game's music triggers give it. A rip's library holds the songs and every MusyX group that the game loads at start-up, 16.2 MB.
- **Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader** (USA): 56 songs. The game streams 60 more. Eight others play a stream along with their notes. GCSF doesn't rip streams. A rip leaves out those eight too. A rip names a song as the game's music hall does, such as `Dogfight_Action`. A song plays at the volume that the hall gives it. Six songs that the hall doesn't list play at MusyX's full volume. The game's code gives no volume for them. A rip's library holds the songs and every bank of the game's sound, 22.8 MB.
- **Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike** (USA): 870 sound effects. A rip names an effect after its ID, such as `RebelStrike-0048`. A rip's library holds the 45 banks of the game's sound that the effects take, 37.6 MB.
- **Pokémon Colosseum** (USA): 77 songs. A rip names a song after its file, such as `title_song`. Each song gets the reverb that the game picks for it. A rip's library holds the songs and the music's two MusyX groups, 5.0 MB.
- **Resident Evil Zero** (USA): 164 songs on two discs. 58 of them are music. 16 of those play in cutscenes. The other 106 are the ambiences of the game's areas. Many cutscenes stream their music. GCSF doesn't rip streams. Each disc holds the songs of its part of the game. A rip of the first disc gives 86 songs, and a rip of the second gives 136. 58 songs are on both. A rip names a song as the game's code does, such as `bgm_00`. A rip's library holds the disc's songs and their MusyX groups: 30.0 MB for the first disc and 40.9 MB for the second. A library's name gives the disc's number, for example `ResidentEvilZeroDisc1.gcsflib`. Rips of both discs can share a folder.
- **Animal Crossing** (USA): 283 pieces of sequenced music and 575 sound effects. They play through the game's port of Nintendo's sound library for the Nintendo 64. The game keeps no names for them. A rip names a piece after the game's number for it, such as `bgm046`, and an effect after its bank and its place in the bank, such as `se2-017`. The 24 pieces that play the hours change with the weather. Each is ripped once for each weather, such as `bgm001-snow`. The museum's piece is ripped once for each room. The music discs' pieces play as they do on a hi-fi stereo. Kapp'n sings his songs on the boat in two parts. Each part is ripped separately. Two more pieces and 14 effects make no sound. The ripper skips them. A rip's library is 7.2 MB.
- **Pokémon Colosseum Bonus Disc** (USA): the 5 sound effects of the program that sends Jirachi to a Game Boy Advance. They play through HAL Laboratory's sound library. The program is a disc image inside the disc. GCSF rips it from there. A rip names an effect after the program's list of names, such as `SFX_cancel`. The program streams its music. GCSF doesn't rip streams.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams.
- The sound effects of Tales of Symphonia, Star Fox Adventures, Rogue Leader, Pokémon Colosseum and Resident Evil Zero aren't ripped. Neither are the jingles of Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door. The game plays them as songs of MusyX. Rebel Strike's songs aren't ripped either.
- A rip plays each of the effects of Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes and Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door with the settings that the game's table gives it. The game places its effects in the world and can change their volumes and pans as they play. A rip plays each of Rebel Strike's effects at the volume and the pan of its group, without the reverb that a level gives it.
- 19 of Rebel Strike's effects jump back through GOTO steps of their macros. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Four of Metroid Prime's effects and one of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes' play only some of the times that the game starts them. In a rip, each one makes a random choice that silences it. The ripper skips it. One more of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes' effects, `SFXspb_a_needlexp_00_oneshot`, tests a variable of MusyX's macros. In a rip, the test skips its sample. The ripper skips the effect.
- Eight of Rogue Leader's songs play a stream along with sequenced notes: The Battle Of Yavin I and III, Hyperspace, Imperial Attack, The Asteroid Field, Celebration & End Titles, and two songs that the music hall doesn't list. A rip of one of them would play only its notes. The ripper leaves them out.
- Some of Resident Evil Zero's cutscene songs start with a rest that times the music to the scene. A rip plays the rest. `bgm_78` is silent for 53 s before its music starts, `bgm_79` for 19 s and `bgm_88` for 16 s.
- Animal Crossing's continuous sounds, such as rivers, the sea and insects, aren't ripped. Neither are the villagers' voices.
- The Bonus Disc's program holds a second set of 825 effects that it never loads. GCSF doesn't rip them.
- 34 of The Wind Waker's effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Each one reads a value that the game sets when it starts the effect, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- 10 of Super Mario Sunshine's effects loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- One of Luigi's Mansion's effects, se2-0239, asks for an instrument that its bank lacks. The game then reads past the end of the bank's list of instruments. Playback of its rip stops with an error at once.
- 181 of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's sound effects loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. 70 of its effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Most of them take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them.
- 872 of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'s sound effects are voices. The game keeps their samples in separate archives and loads one of those at a time. A rip loads none of them. These effects play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them. 32 of the game's effects, such as the engines and the crowds, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star's and the Chain Chomp's, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Three of Pikmin 2's effects play only some of the times that the game starts them: a Wollywog's voice and two of the Giant Breadbug's sounds. In a rip, each one draws a random number that silences it, and the ripper skips it.
- Twilight Princess HD lacks one of the Wii's wave archives. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
