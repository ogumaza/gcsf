GCSF 1.4.0 rips one more game: Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles. The player, the foobar2000 component and the XMPlay plugin of GCSF 1.3.0 play every rip that 1.4.0 makes.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game code on an emulated GameCube: the OS, the sound library and the DSP program of the game. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `gcsf-1.4.0-win-x64.zip` | `gcsfrip` and `gcsfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_gcsf-1.4.0.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-gcsf-1.4.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.4.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New games

- **Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles** (USA): 59 pieces of sequenced music and 2,136 sound effects. Both play through Square's Red driver. A 60th piece and 262 of the effects make no sound. The ripper skips them. Most of those effects start no note. A rip names a piece after its file, such as `music100`, and an effect after its file, such as `se010001`. An effect in one of the four blocks of effects of the game is named after the block and its place in the block, such as `se000-001`. The library of a rip (`.gcsflib`) contains the files of the music and the effects, 117.1 MB. The game streams 21 more pieces. GCSF doesn't rip streams.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams.
- 34 effects of The Wind Waker play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Each one reads a value that the game sets when it starts the effect, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- 10 effects of Super Mario Sunshine loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- One effect of Luigi's Mansion, se2-0239, requests an instrument that is missing from its bank. The game then reads past the end of the instrument list of the bank. Playback of its rip stops with an error at once.
- 181 sound effects of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. 70 of its effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Most of them take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them.
- 872 sound effects of Mario Kart: Double Dash!! are voices. The game keeps their samples in separate archives and loads one of those at a time. A rip loads none of them. These effects play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them. 32 effects of the game, such as the engines and the crowds, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star music and the Chain Chomp music, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Three effects of Pikmin 2 play only some of the times that the game starts them: the voice of a Wollywog and two sounds of the Giant Breadbug. In a rip, each one draws a random number that silences it, and the ripper skips it.
- Twilight Princess HD is missing one of the wave archives of the Wii release. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
