GCSF 1.3.0 rips ten more games: Mario Power Tennis, WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!, Luigi's Mansion, Super Mario Sunshine, Pikmin, Donkey Kong Jungle Beat, Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, Pikmin 2, Pac-Man Vs. and The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game's code on an emulated GameCube: the game's OS, its sound library and its DSP program. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `gcsf-1.3.0-win-x64.zip` | `gcsfrip` and `gcsfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_gcsf-1.3.0.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-gcsf-1.3.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.3.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New games

- **Mario Power Tennis** (USA): its 111 songs and jingles. Like Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour, the game plays its music through Factor 5's sound library, MusyX. The game's sound effects aren't ripped.
- **WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!** (USA): the 231 pieces of music in its Sound Room, with the Sound Room's names. The game plays them through MusyX, under Intelligent Systems' sound code. Two of the pieces only count beats for the game. They make no sound, and the ripper skips them. The game's sound effects aren't ripped.
- **Luigi's Mansion** (USA): its 84 pieces of music and 1,135 sound effects. The game plays them through an early version of JAudio1, the sound library of The Wind Waker. The ripper skips 4 pieces and 128 effects that make no sound. Two more pieces are streams (`.afc`). GCSF doesn't rip streams.
- **Super Mario Sunshine** (USA): its 47 pieces of music and 1,679 sound effects, under the names that the game gives them, such as `MSD_BGM_DOLPIC`. The game plays them through JAudio1, the sound library of The Wind Waker. The ripper skips 255 effects that make no sound. One more piece is a stream (`title.afc`).
- **Pikmin** (USA): 13 pieces of music, under the names of their files, such as `select`, and 8 parts of the music of its cutscenes, such as `demobgm-5`. The game plays them through an early version of JAudio1, the sound library of The Wind Waker. The ripper skips a piece that makes no sound. 13 more pieces, most of them for the game's cutscenes, are streams (`.stx`). GCSF doesn't rip streams. The game's sound effects aren't ripped.
- **Donkey Kong Jungle Beat** (USA): its 83 pieces of music and its sound effects. The game plays them through JAudio2. Four Swords Adventures and Twilight Princess use it too. Each piece of music starts as the game starts it, with the tracks that the game first turns on.
- **Mario Kart: Double Dash!!** (USA): its 12 sequenced pieces of music and its sound effects. The game plays them through the earliest known version of JAudio2. The courses' music is streamed. GCSF doesn't rip streams. Sounds that the game keeps alive frame by frame, such as the karts' engines, play on in a rip until they end by themselves.
- **Pikmin 2** (USA): 47 sequenced pieces of music, named after their files, and 1,034 sound effects, under the names that the game gives them, such as `PSSE_SY_MENU_CURSOR`. The game's music changes with the action. A rip plays each piece as it sounds when nothing is going on. Each piece of the caves' music has a conductor. The game picks one for each floor, and the conductor picks the phrases at random. A rip plays a cave's piece with the first conductor of its family. A cave's piece never repeats, and its rip plays for ten minutes before the fade. The bosses' music starts where the game starts it when a boss appears. The ripper skips 4 effects that make no sound. The game's streamed music (`.ast`) isn't ripped.
- **Pac-Man Vs.** (USA): 59 sound effects, named after their categories and indexes, such as `se1-0000`. The game streams its music. Its effects play through the build of JAudio that Pikmin 2 uses. 17 effects make no sound. The ripper skips them. Five of them are empty, 11 are narration without a sample, and one has a volume of 0 in the game's table.
- **The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition** (USA): the 23 sound effects of the disc's menu and one piece of music, `JA_BGM_DUMMY`. The rips have the names that the menu's sound table gives them, such as `JA_SE_TR_OC_CURSOR_L`. The menu streams its music. Its code never starts `JA_BGM_DUMMY`. The disc's other programs aren't ripped: the four games that it emulates and a demo of The Wind Waker.

## Other changes

- **Code page 932 on discs.** `gcsfrip` decodes a disc's Shift-JIS text with code page 932, Windows' version of Shift-JIS. Circled digits, Roman numerals and the other characters that Windows adds now decode. They used to come out as `%XX`. Seven symbols, such as the wave dash, now decode as they do on Windows.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams.
- 34 of The Wind Waker's effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Each one reads a value that the game sets when it starts the effect, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- 10 of Super Mario Sunshine's effects loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- One of Luigi's Mansion's effects, se2-0239, asks for an instrument that its bank lacks. The game then reads past the end of the bank's list of instruments. Playback of its rip stops with an error at once.
- 181 of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's sound effects loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. 70 of its effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Most of them take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them.
- 872 of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'s sound effects are voices. The game keeps their samples in separate archives and loads one of those at a time. A rip loads none of them. These effects play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them. 32 of the game's effects, such as the engines and the crowds, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star's and the Chain Chomp's, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Three of Pikmin 2's effects play only some of the times that the game starts them: a Wollywog's voice and two of the Giant Breadbug's sounds. In a rip, each one draws a random number that silences it, and the ripper skips it.
- Twilight Princess HD lacks one of the Wii's wave archives. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
