GCSF 1.7.0 rips the songs that Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door plays as sounds. GCSF also rips the sound effects of Pikmin, Pokémon Colosseum, WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$! and the NDDEMO tech demo. The rips of Mario Party 4 now include its sound effects and voices. The rips of Mario Kart: Double Dash!! now include its voices. The songs of Mario Power Tennis now start as they do in the game. The library of a rip now contains zeros in place of the files that none of its sounds reads. Other rips play as they did with 1.6.1.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game code on an emulated GameCube: the OS, the sound library and the DSP program of the game. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `gcsf-1.7.0-win-x64.zip` | `gcsfrip` and `gcsfplay` for 64-bit Windows |
| `foo_input_gcsf-1.7.0.fb2k-component` | the foobar2000 component, for 64-bit and 32-bit foobar2000 v2 |
| `xmp-gcsf-1.7.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.7.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New in 1.7.0

- **The songs of Paper Mario.** Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door plays MusyX songs as sounds, among them its jingles. GCSF now rips those songs. They come after the effects. A rip names a song as the table of sounds in the game does, for example `SFX_SE2_EVT_STARSTONE_GET1`. A song that the table doesn't list gets its name from the list of songs in the game, for example `SE1_TEST`. `--bgm` rips the songs alone.
- **The sound effects of Pikmin.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of Pikmin. The ripper lists the effects in the catalogue of the game and the few effects that the code of the game plays outside it. A rip starts each effect as the game does: as an effect of the system, as an effect of the player, or as an action of an event at the camera. A rip names an effect as the catalogue does, for example `SE_FLOG_JUMP`.
- **The sound effects of Pokémon Colosseum.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of Pokémon Colosseum. They follow the songs. A rip starts each effect as the game does. A rip names an effect after its set and its ID, for example `snd_se_battle-0202`.
- **The sound effects of WarioWare.** WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$! plays most of its sound effects as MusyX songs. GCSF now rips them and the other effects of the game. A rip names an effect after the number that the game gives it, for example `se-0013`.
- **The sound effects of the NDDEMO tech demo.** GCSF now rips the sound effects of the NDDEMO tech demo. A rip starts each effect as the demo does. An effect keeps the name that the demo gives it, for example `SFXcoin_oneshot`.
- **The sound effects and voices of Mario Party 4.** GCSF now rips the sound effects and voices of Mario Party 4. They follow the songs. A rip names an effect after its number in the table of effects of the game, for example `se-0042`. The Option Room names some of the voices. A rip adds such a name, for example `se-0054 Toad Happy`.
- **The voices of Double Dash!!.** Mario Kart: Double Dash!! keeps the voices of the characters in separate archives and loads one of them at a time. A rip now loads the archive that contains the bank of a voice. The voices now rip.

## Fixed in 1.7.0

- **An effect that stopped its player.** One effect of Luigi's Mansion, se2-0239, selects an instrument that is missing from its bank. The game then reads past the end of the instrument list of the bank. The emulated machine of GCSF stopped there. Playback of the rip stopped with an error at once. When the machine stops with an error during the length analysis, the ripper now plays the sound again from its start. A sound that makes no sound before the stop counts as silent. The ripper skips it with the other silent sounds. The ripper prints the error of the emulation. The ripper now skips se2-0239 in this way.
- **The starts of the songs in Mario Power Tennis.** The sound set-up of Mario Power Tennis fades the two master volumes of MusyX down. The game plays no music before the fade has ended. A rip started each song during the fade. The start of each song therefore played too loudly. A rip now waits for the fade to end before it starts a song.

## Other changes

- **Smaller libraries.** For most MusyX games, the library of a rip contained every set and every song of the game's catalogue. For Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles, the library contained every piece, every block of effects and every bank. For Pikmin, the library contained every wave archive. A set, song, file or wave archive that no sound of the rip reads now contains only zeros. Each file keeps its size. The file table of the disc and the places of the files stay the same. The zeros compress well. A rip made with `--bgm` or `--only` therefore takes less space. The rips play as before.
- **Two rips into one folder.** A library now depends on the sounds that its rip contains. A `.gcsflib` that differs from one of the same name in the folder therefore gets the next free name, such as `<name>_2.gcsflib`. The rips already in the folder then still play. A library with the same contents keeps its name. To replace earlier rips, rip into an empty folder.
- **The music of Rebel Strike.** Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike streams all of its music. The README and `docs/gcsf.md` now say so. Earlier notes listed the game's songs as not ripped. A rip of the game contains the sound effects.
- **The libraries.** GCSF now builds on xsflib 1.6.0, with ntdlib 1.11.0.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams. Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike streams all of its music.
- The sound effects of Tales of Symphonia, Star Fox Adventures, Rogue Leader, Resident Evil Zero, Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour, Mario Power Tennis and Nintendo Puzzle Collection aren't ripped.
- A rip plays each effect of Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes and Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door with the settings that the game's table gives it. The game places its effects in the world and can change their volumes and pans as they play. A rip plays each effect of Rebel Strike at the volume and the pan of its group, without the reverb that a level gives it.
- 19 effects of Rebel Strike jump back through GOTO steps of their macros. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Four effects of Metroid Prime and one of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes play only some of the times that the game starts them. In a rip, each one makes a random choice that silences it. The ripper skips it. One more effect of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes, `SFXspb_a_needlexp_00_oneshot`, tests a variable of the MusyX macros. In a rip, the test skips its sample. The ripper skips the effect.
- Eight songs of Rogue Leader play a stream along with sequenced notes: The Battle Of Yavin I and III, Hyperspace, Imperial Attack, The Asteroid Field, Celebration & End Titles, and two songs that the music hall doesn't list. A rip of one of them would play only its notes. The ripper leaves them out.
- Some cutscene songs of Resident Evil Zero start with a rest that times the music to the scene. A rip plays the rest. `bgm_78` is silent for 53 s before its music starts, `bgm_79` for 19 s and `bgm_88` for 16 s.
- The continuous sounds of Animal Crossing, such as rivers, the sea and insects, aren't ripped. Neither are the villagers' voices.
- The program on the Bonus Disc contains a second set of 825 effects that it never loads. GCSF doesn't rip them.
- 34 effects of The Wind Waker play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Each one reads a value that the game sets when it starts the effect, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- 10 effects of Super Mario Sunshine loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- 181 sound effects of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. 70 of its effects play nothing in a rip, and the ripper skips them. Most of them take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them.
- 32 sound effects of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, such as the engines and the crowds, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star music and the Chain Chomp music, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Three effects of Pikmin 2 play only some of the times that the game starts them: the voice of a Wollywog and two sounds of the Giant Breadbug. In a rip, each one draws a random number that silences it, and the ripper skips it.
- Some effects of Pikmin loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade. Some other sounds of Pikmin, such as those of the formation, follow the state of the game through values that the game sets. The ripper leaves them out.
- Twilight Princess HD is missing one of the wave archives of the Wii release. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
