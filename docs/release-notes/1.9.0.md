GCSF 1.9.0 rips the songs of Resident Evil. It also rips the European release of Four Swords Adventures, the European and Japanese releases of Twilight Princess, the Japanese release of The Wind Waker, disc version 0 of Doubutsu no Mori+ and Doubutsu no Mori e+, and the other releases of Mario Party 4. The music of The Wind Waker, Twilight Princess and Pikmin 2 now starts in the state that the game sets. A rip contains each other state of that music as a variant. The cutscene effects of Resident Evil Zero now rip too. Other rips play as they did with 1.8.1.

GCSF rips Nintendo GameCube sequenced music into a PSF-family format. A rip plays with the game code on an emulated GameCube: the OS, the sound library and the DSP program of the game. The release comes with a ripper (`gcsfrip`), a renderer (`gcsfplay`), a foobar2000 component and an XMPlay plugin. `docs/gcsf.md` describes the format.

| `xmp-gcsf-1.9.0.zip` | the XMPlay plugin, for XMPlay 3.8.2 or later |

The programs run on Windows 10 or later and require no runtime DLLs. To install the component, open the file with foobar2000, or add it under Preferences › Components. To install the XMPlay plugin, unzip `xmp-gcsf-1.9.0.zip` and put `xmp-gcsf.dll` in XMPlay's folder, or in a folder inside it. Then restart XMPlay. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New in 1.9.0

- **Resident Evil.** GCSF now rips the songs of Resident Evil (USA). The first disc contains them, and the second disc contains the same files. A rip names a song after its file, for example `sg00`. Files that repeat another file, and songs that make no sound, are left out. The code of a room changes the channel levels of some songs while they play. A rip plays each song at the levels of its file. Six more rips play the mixes that rooms set for two of the ambiences, for example `sg74 ch1` and `sg7a ch0-3+5`.
- **More releases.** GCSF now rips the European release of The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures, the European and Japanese releases of The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess, and the Japanese release of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker, Zelda no Densetsu: Kaze no Takuto. Their rips play and are named as the American rips are. It also rips disc version 0 of Doubutsu no Mori+ and Doubutsu no Mori e+, and disc version 0 of Mario Party 4 (USA), its European release and its Japanese release. The Japanese release of Mario Party 4 names its songs and voices in Japanese like its sound test, for example `いろいろためそう`.
- **The cutscene effects of Resident Evil Zero.** 18 cutscenes of the game play their sound effects as songs. GCSF now rips them. A rip names one after its cutscene and its song ID, for example `dd01-0413`.
- **The music of The Wind Waker in the states of the game.** The sound layer of the game starts its music by the state of the game, such as the sail of the boat, Link's sword or an enemy near Link. It mutes some tracks of 14 pieces as it starts them. Some IDs of the game start a piece in another arrangement. A rip now starts each piece as the game starts it. It also contains each other arrangement, named after the piece and the ID or the state, for example `kaminoto 80000125` or `sea sailing`. The battle music and three other pieces fade in over two seconds. The game fades them in too. While some pieces play, the game writes a status to them, such as the phase of a boss fight. A rip contains such a piece once more for each status that changes what it plays, for example `p_ganon1 9=2`.
- **The random effects of The Wind Waker.** Four effects of the game pick the key of their note at random. They made no sound in a rip. A rip now contains each of them once for each key that makes a sound, for example `se4-0254 key 48`.
- **The music of Twilight Princess in the states of the game.** The game mutes some tracks of five pieces as it starts them. A rip now mutes the same tracks. The day music of Hyrule Field plays one arrangement while Link walks and another while he rides. A rip contains both: `Z2BGM_FIELD_LINK_DAY` and `Z2BGM_FIELD_LINK_DAY_riding`. The scenes and fights of the game give 17 pieces more states. A rip contains each other state, named after the piece and the state, for example `Z2BGM_TOAL_VILLEGE_status1`. The day music of Hyrule Field also follows the code of the field. A rip contains each way through the piece, in each arrangement.
- **A second set of tracks in Four Swords Adventures.** The game plays `JA_BGM_SELECT` with one set of its tracks on the file select and another on the map where the players pick a stage. A rip now contains both: `JA_BGM_SELECT` and `JA_BGM_SELECT map`.
- **The caves and bosses of Pikmin 2.** The music of a cave changes with the floor, and the music of a boss with the actions of the boss. `gcsfrip` now rips a variant of the piece for the conductor of each other floor, for example `caveconc_05_0`, and for each other part of the music of a boss, for example `m_boss-3`. Two parts of the music of the final boss have a layer that plays only while a captain stands near the boss. They get one more variant each, labelled `-near`.
- **LuigiSings while Luigi is shrunk.** A poison mushroom shrinks Luigi, and the tune then plays an octave higher. A rip now contains that state in each place, with "shrunk" at the end of its name.

## Fixed in 1.9.0

- **The final boss music of Pikmin 2.** The game sets up the music of the final boss with the layers of the floor of the boss. A rip played the music without them, and four of its parts lost a layer. A rip now plays every part with its layers.
- **The random effects of Pikmin 2.** The voice of a Wollywog and two sounds of the Giant Breadbug play only on some of the random numbers that the game draws. In a rip, the first number silenced them, and the ripper skipped them. A rip now plays each one with the first number on which it sounds.
- **The level sounds of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!.** The game starts a level sound again each frame. Six of its level sounds finish each pass, and the next start runs them again. Their rips played for ten minutes. They now loop after two passes.
- **The effects of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat.** Some effects of the game add a value that the game gives them to the key of a note. A rip now starts such an effect with the value that the game gives it first. The clap of the pause menu, for one, now plays a note. The effect that plays the tones of a combo now plays over the music of a stage. It made no sound before. Ten chord effects whose tracks all start at once made no sound in a rip. They now play.
- **Fade-ins of MusyX songs.** Star Fox Adventures raises every song to its volume over half a second. The cutscenes of Resident Evil Zero raise their songs over 133 ms. A rip started each song at its volume at once. It now fades in as the game does.

## Other changes

- GCSF emulates the DSP and the CPU faster. Rips play more easily in real time on slower computers.
- At the start and after a seek, the foobar2000 component and the XMPlay plugin now wait until three seconds are rendered ahead before they play.

## Known limitations

- Only games with a driver profile can be ripped.
- Sounds that play until the game stops them, such as ambiences, get two loops and a fade when the ripper finds their loops. A sound that settles on a held note loops there. Other sounds play until their notes end, for at most ten minutes.
- Only sequenced sound is ripped. Use tools such as vgmstream for streams (`.ast`, `.adp`, `.str`). Navi Trackers, a mode that only Four Swords Plus has, plays its music from streams. Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike streams all of its music.
- The ripper leaves out the effects of one voice file of Tales of Symphonia. That file contains no pool of samples. MusyX can't load its group.
- GCSF doesn't rip the sound effects of Resident Evil. The code of a room changes the channel levels of some songs while they play, above all those of the ambiences. A rip plays each song at the levels of its file, apart from the six mixes.
- A rip plays each effect of Metroid Prime, Metroid Prime 2: Echoes and Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door with the settings that the game's table gives it. The game places its effects in the world and can change their volumes and pans as they play. A rip plays each effect of Rebel Strike at the volume and the pan of its group, without the reverb that a level gives it.
- The code of Tales of Symphonia and Rogue Leader can give an effect a pan. A rip plays each of their effects in the centre. Star Fox Adventures sends its effects to a reverb in some places of its world. Resident Evil Zero sends its effects to an aux bus. A rip plays the effects of both games without those sends.
- 19 effects of Rebel Strike jump back through GOTO steps of their macros. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Some effects of Tales of Symphonia and Rogue Leader keep going until the game stops them. The ripper finds no loop in them. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade.
- Four effects of Metroid Prime and one of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes play only some of the times that the game starts them. In a rip, each one makes a random choice that silences it. The ripper skips it. One more effect of Metroid Prime 2: Echoes, `SFXspb_a_needlexp_00_oneshot`, tests a variable of the MusyX macros. In a rip, the test skips its sample. The ripper skips the effect.
- Eight songs of Rogue Leader play a stream along with sequenced notes: The Battle Of Yavin I and III, Hyperspace, Imperial Attack, The Asteroid Field, Celebration & End Titles, and two songs that the music hall doesn't list. A rip of one of them would play only its notes. The ripper leaves them out.
- Some cutscene songs of Resident Evil Zero start with a rest that times the music to the scene. A rip plays the rest. `bgm_78` is silent for 53 s before its music starts, `bgm_79` for 19 s and `bgm_88` for 16 s.
- The continuous sounds of Animal Crossing and its Japanese releases, such as rivers, the sea and insects, aren't ripped. Neither are the villagers' voices.
- The program on the Bonus Disc contains a second set of 825 effects that it never loads. GCSF doesn't rip them.
- Some effects of The Wind Waker read the reverb of the room that they play in. A few of them make no sound in a rip with any value. The ripper skips them. `docs/gcsf.md` lists them.
- A rip plays `LuigiSings` in 17 states of Luigi's Mansion. Each one takes the most health of its band. In the game, the tempo falls further as Luigi loses health within a band. While the lights are on, the tempo follows Luigi's steps. A rip of the lit room plays the tempo of Luigi standing still.
- 10 effects of Super Mario Sunshine loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes.
- 181 sound effects of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat loop, but the ripper doesn't find their loops. They play for ten minutes, then fade. The keys of a chord effect depend on the stage, the chord of the moment and the keys before. A rip plays each chord effect in one state: over the music of one stage, from the first of the game's records that names it. Some chord effects play no notes in that state. The ripper skips them. Some others repeat their steps until the game stops them. They play for ten minutes, then fade. Later in a combo, the game moves some chord effects on by more steps. A rip plays only the steps that start by themselves. Some effects that aren't chord effects take their notes from values that the game sets as it plays them. They play nothing in a rip. The ripper skips them.
- 16 level sounds of Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, the ramps of the engines, play for ten minutes, then fade. The ripper doesn't find their loops. Two pieces of music, the star music and the Chain Chomp music, loop every 6 and 11 seconds but play for 4 min 27 s before the fade.
- Some effects of Pikmin loop inside a subroutine. The ripper doesn't find those loops. Their rips play for ten minutes, then fade. A rip plays the formation sound for one move only: a full move straight ahead. The chatter of the Pikmin follows the state of the game through values that the game sets. The ripper leaves it out.
- Twilight Princess HD is missing one of the wave archives of the Wii release. `gcsfrip` warns about it. The sounds that use it play without its waves.
- The XMPlay plugin and the 32-bit foobar2000 component run the emulation more slowly than the 64-bit programs. On a slow computer, the busiest pieces may not play in real time in them.
- The output is stereo. Surround isn't played.
